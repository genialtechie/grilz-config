# VVS Lab redesign: round 1 moodboard

Live board: https://claude.ai/artifact/BChkYV5CTks8eKQwDSngEf (private; share from its Share menu).

Scope: landing page, builder (configurator) UX, and the VVS Lab brand. Magpollo stays
a legal footnote ("a Magpollo product"), never a header or builder logo.

## The brief in one line

Two audiences, one stage: the landing page wins the **jeweler** (buyer, $199/mo);
the builder wins the **jeweler's customer**, in the jeweler's brand. Both should open
with the object (desire), not a form (data).

## Audit (vvslab.magpollo.com, Nov 2026)

- Landing: sharp hook in the trade's words; hero is a still image of a live 3D product;
  no proof (shops, faces, quotes, numbers); on phones the image pushes the headline below the fold.
- Builder: solid mechanics (MAG-95/148); step 1 is tooth counting with three control groups;
  plaster model under flat light (gold reads brassy); competing CTAs; price shown before desire;
  different brand from the landing page; Magpollo logo in the customer's header.

## Direction

- **Landing, 8 beats:** live 3D hero, DM before/after, 3D-to-photo reveal, "see it with your
  shop name", the lead card on the jeweler's phone, proof from the trade, their-own-numbers maths,
  pricing/FAQ/bio-link close.
- **Builder, 6 steps:** pick a look, teeth (flat tooth map plus chips), metal, finish (options
  rendered on their set), extras, reveal (photo develops while they type, then breakdown,
  deposit, 9:16 share card). Mechanics: every step pre-answered, the camera follows the choice,
  a growing spec receipt, price in a pill, "text me this design", a share card.
- **Brand:** Gold & Ice palette (Ink, Bone, Gold, Ice; red kept only as Signal); territory
  "The Bench" leads (Chrome Gothic for social, The Lab for the dashboard); Bodoni Moda display
  plus a mono for specs; motifs: hallmark stamp, clarity scale, tooth map, glint. Karat marks
  moments on VVS surfaces, never inside a shop's builder.

## Open decisions (recommendation in brackets)

1. Accent colour: red or gold & ice? [gold & ice, red as Signal]
2. Landing hero: playable 3D or film? [poster that becomes playable; tooth map on phones]
3. Builder entry: looks first or teeth first? [looks first, "Start blank" one tap away]
4. Price: always on or revealed? [quiet pill throughout, big number on reveal; per-shop setting]
5. Proof: 3 jewelers on record? [yes, before the landing rebuild ships]
6. Territory: Bench, Chrome Gothic or Lab? [Bench leads]
