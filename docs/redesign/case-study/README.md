# Case study material

Screenshots of each prototype version, kept for the portfolio case study of the
VVS Lab redesign. Files are named `date-version-what.jpg`, by the day each was
captured (UTC). The written notes (timeline, decisions and why, craft notes,
numbers to capture) are in the "Case study notes" tab of the plan page, a
private Claude artifact.

| Version | Date | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| v1 | Nov 14 | Blank canvas: the piece alone on a stage, front door and builder on one screen; three room options (Stone was chosen) |
| v2 | Nov 15 | Teeth sculpted in code, jeweler's light for metal, mouth open and close |
| v3 | Nov 15 | Clear resin holder, set stones in bezels, the first one-of-one charms; v3.1 packs smaller stones in a thin rim |
| v5 | Nov 15 | The full journey: front door, five-step builder, review, send and deposit, jeweler sign-up, phone |
| teeth 1-4 | Nov 15-16 | Rebuilding the teeth: the live model as stored, levelled, then smoothed and seated with the bite closed; before and after |
| v6 | Nov 16 | Real tooth shapes and spacing across every finish, setting, charm, view and phone |
| creator 5 | Nov 16 | The overhauled builder's menu after three passes with the owner: round pictures of each choice, no arrows or borders, one type family (desktop and phone) |
| creator 6 | Nov 17 | Start empty opens Your build; Next leads through every part with Back beside it; stages on top; Undo on the guide's question (phone and desktop, 8 steps each) |
| creator 7 | Nov 17 | The three screens after Done: Your grill, Where should VVS Lab text you (code step, returning shopper), Sent (the studio photo develops from the 3D, deposit, extras, what happens next) |
| port 1-2 | Nov 16 | The approved piece inside the real builder (iced; a per-tooth mix); every state next to the prototype is in `docs/redesign/port/screenshots/` |

Only our own work is here. Reference images (competitor sites, games, the
owner's reference photos) were used during the work but are not kept, as
they belong to others.
