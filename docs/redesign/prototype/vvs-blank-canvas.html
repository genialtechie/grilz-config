This round

What changed in the 3D.

The plan, decisions and what comes next live in the redesign plan.

A clear holder

The grill sits on a clear resin mold, like the one in your reference photos. It starts at 70% solid; the dial on the stage changes how see-through it is.

Rounder teeth that touch

Every tooth is one smooth form with its own shape for front teeth, canines and back teeth. Neighbours meet side to side without running into each other.

Cream first, metal second

Picked teeth show in the cream material on the Teeth step. The real metal appears when you move to Metal.

Real set stones

Iced teeth carry separate cut stones in a honeycomb inside a gold rim. A raised gold bezel frames them, as in your reference photos.

One-of-one charms

Eight original charms can go on any tooth: a crest, a crown, a cross, a spade, a bolt, a wing and two bezel-set stones. They are premium, so they show as a quote from the shop.

One tooth at a time

On Metal, Finish and Custom, tap a tooth to change just that one. It lifts forward and the camera turns to face it. Tap empty space to go back to all teeth.

Things to try

Five minutes with it.

  1. Press "See a one-of-one" on the front door.A full showpiece: iced canines in gold bezels, a cross, crest, crown and spade on top, onyx and diamond cut below.
  2. Slide the holder from 30% to 100% solid.Find the point where it looks right to you.
  3. Go to Finish, tap one tooth, and make it iced.Then turn the piece slowly and watch the stones.
  4. Open the mouth and look from the side.Check the tooth shapes: front, canine and back.
