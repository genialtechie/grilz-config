Questions

Getting grillz

What metals can I choose?

Yellow, white or rose gold in 10K, 14K or 18K, sterling silver, or platinum. You pick in the builder and watch the price change.

How do you get the shape of my teeth?

We post you a mold kit: you bite, then mail it back. Or send your own mold or a scan.

Is the price I see final?

It's an estimate from the real prices. The shop confirms the exact price after your fitting.

What does sending my design cost?

Nothing. You send it free and the shop texts you.

What's the deposit?

An optional payment that starts your order. You see the amount before you pay, and it counts toward your grill.

Do I need an account?

No. Designing is free. We only ask for your phone when you send a design or use AI, and we text you a code.

Selling grillz

Do I need a website?

No. The builder is its own link, so it works from your Instagram bio, a story or a DM. If you have a site, it can sit there too.

Whose prices does it show?

Yours. You set your metal, stone and per-tooth prices, so the total a customer sees matches what you would quote.

Where does the deposit go?

Through your own Stripe account, straight to you. VVS Lab never holds your money.

Is the studio photo the finished piece?

It is a photo-real picture of the design they built, so they can see it before paying. The 3D build and the spec you receive are what you work from at the bench.

Can customers ask for a one-of-one?

Yes. They can place charms on any tooth or send their own idea with a photo. Those arrive as a custom request for you to quote.

Is there a contract?

No. 14 days free with no card, then $199 a month. Cancel any month.
